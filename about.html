WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Golen Mixed Farm

Golen Mixed Farm is an agricultural enterprise dedicated to supplying quality farm products to households, restaurants and businesses across Uganda.

We offer a range of farm products including eggs, honey, grains, flour, fruits, vegetables and other farm produce depending on availability.

Our goal is to make quality farm products accessible while building lasting relationships with our customers and partners.
